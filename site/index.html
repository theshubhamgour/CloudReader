Features

Everything you need to listen.
Nothing you don't.

Built for people who'd rather hear their reading than stare at it: writers checking a draft, students, commuters, or anyone with a long document.

Any document

Word, EPUB, plain text and Markdown. Chapters are found automatically from your headings.

Plays in seconds

Press Play and listening starts almost immediately, while the rest of the book is prepared in the background.

Natural AI voices

Use the free built-in voice, or add a Gemini key for expressive, human-like narration with 30 voices.

Bookmarks & resume

CloudReader remembers exactly where you stopped. Bookmark a moment and jump back with one tap.

Desktop & phone

A clean player on your computer, and on your phone over your home Wi-Fi. Add it to your home screen like an app.

Download & keep

Export any chapter as MP3, or the whole audiobook as a ZIP, and listen anywhere.

How it works

Three steps. That's it.

Add a document

Drop a file onto the Library, or click Choose File. It becomes a book with chapters.

Press Play

Narration starts within seconds and keeps going ahead of you while you listen.

Listen anywhere

Pause, resume, bookmark, change speed, or download it for later.

Voices

Free by default.
Beautiful when you want it.

Switch between voices any time in Settings. No account is needed to get started.

Local Voice

Free · Default
  • Works right away, with no setup
  • Runs offline, on your computer
  • Nothing ever leaves your device
  • Unlimited and always free

Uses your Mac's built-in speech voices.

Gemini AI Voice

Bring your own key
  • Natural, expressive narration
  • 30 voices: warm, bright, calm and more
  • Choose Fast or Best Quality
  • Hear a sample before you choose

Needs a free Google Gemini API key. The free tier allows a few requests a day; whole books need billing, at roughly $0.50–0.80 per hour of audio.

Your books stay yours.

  • Everything (your library, audio and listening progress) is stored on your computer.
  • No accounts, no analytics, no tracking.
  • With the Local Voice, nothing is ever sent anywhere.
  • With Gemini, only the text being read aloud is sent to Google, and your key stays in your system keychain.
Get started

Up and running in a minute.

Open Terminal and paste this. The first run sets everything up, then CloudReader opens in your browser.

$ git clone https://github.com/theshubhamgour/CloudReader.git
$ cd CloudReader
$ ./start.sh
# Optional: also open it on your phone (same Wi-Fi)
$ ./start.sh --phone

Needs macOS (recommended) or Linux with Python 3.11+, Node.js 20+ and FFmpeg.
On a Mac, the start script offers to install anything that's missing.

Questions

Is CloudReader really free?

Yes. CloudReader is free and open source under the MIT license, and the Local Voice costs nothing. Only the optional Gemini AI voice is billed, by Google and to your own API key, if you go beyond its free tier.

Which file types can I use?

Word (.docx), EPUB, plain text (.txt) and Markdown (.md), up to 50 MB. PDF isn't supported yet; export to DOCX or EPUB first.

Do I need an internet connection?

Not with the Local Voice. Everything runs on your computer. The Gemini voice needs an internet connection to reach Google.

Does it work on Windows?

CloudReader is built for macOS and also runs on Linux. The Local Voice uses macOS speech; on other systems, use the Gemini voice. Windows isn't supported yet.

Can I listen on my phone?

Yes. Start it with ./start.sh --phone and open the address it prints on a phone connected to the same Wi-Fi. You can add it to your home screen to open it like an app.

Is my writing sent anywhere?

Not with the Local Voice. With the Gemini voice, only the passage being read aloud is sent to Google. On Gemini's free tier, Google may use that text to improve its products, so use a billed key for unpublished work.
